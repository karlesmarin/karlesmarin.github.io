Abstract

Revised Part I of How Transformers Attend (19 November 2026). The 129-page English paper and 140-page Spanish translation distinguish measured attention profiles, conditional finite power-law calculations, configuration candidates and task outcomes. The Spanish edition uses Babel; its 2,272 mathematical spans, labels, references and numerical items were checked against the English source.

The paper integrates eight groups of conditional mathematical results. The accompanying Isabelle source verifies stated mathematical implications; Lean covers parts of four body results. These proofs do not establish a universal learned attention exponent, circuit causality or quality after KV compression. Historical interventions and instrument biases are reported with their limits.

This version deposits both full-paper PDFs, a separate Spanish theorem note, SHA-256 receipts and one source/data/program/proof archive (840 files, including 198 programs and 423 data files). A historical D3 result JSON is unavailable and is explicitly not reconstructed. The companion Blueprint maps the statements and four illustrative labs. The field guide gives measurement and application routes; TAF Agent calculates candidates or imports protocol-bound observations. The author remains responsible for the research and claims.
