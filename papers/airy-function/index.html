Abstract

A sorry-free Lean 4 development of the Airy function from a three-line recurrence to the quantum-bouncer spectrum, depending only on the three standard axioms of classical Mathlib (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound). From c_{k+3}=c_k/((k+2)(k+3)), c_2=0: the generated power series is entire; its sum F satisfies the Airy ODE F''(x)=x F(x) as functions on all of R; on the negative axis F oscillates (infinitely many zeros, by a Sturm comparison built from Rolle and the intermediate value theorem, since Mathlib has no Sturm–Liouville theory); on the positive axis the dominant solutions escape and the recessive (decaying) solution is constructed by reduction of order, Ai_rec(x)=f(x) * integral_x^inf f(t)^-2 dt, with no DLMF asymptotics, then extended to an entire Airy function; and the quantum bouncer (a neutron above a mirror in gravity, GRANIT / qBounce) has its energy spectrum at the negatives of the Airy zeros, with infinitely many levels. The attached archive contains the self-contained Lean source (Airy.lean, AiryReal.lean) and a mpmath/SageMath cross-check. A large language model was used as a coding assistant; every statement was independently checked by the Lean kernel, and all mathematical decisions and claims are the author's.
