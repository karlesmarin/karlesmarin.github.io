A divisor-sum criterion for Kassel--Reutenauer polynomial divisibility modulo odd primes

Carles Marín Muñoz

2026-11-27Version 1DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24573486CC BY 4.0

💠 Number theory & characters

📕PDF🔗DOI🗄️Zenodo

Abstract

We give an exact divisor-sum criterion for the integers n such that p divides the sum of divisors of n but the Kassel--Reutenauer polynomial P_n(q) is not divisible by [p]_q over F_p[q]. We classify the odd primes for which the implication holds for every n and construct infinitely many exceptions for every other prime. The note includes complete proofs, exact computational checks, and an illustrated counting question.

In response to the problem posed by J. M. Rodríguez Caballero. The author used AI assistance in the research and preparation. No external human peer review is claimed.
