Abstract

The novelty claim of this Letter is WITHDRAWN. Nothing in this record is offered as a new result. Version 4.0 changes no text and no conclusion: it brings the three documents of the story together into one version, in reading order, because until now each version held only its own fragment and the concept DOI resolved to the retraction alone.

Read in this order:

  1. 1_Letter_en.pdf / 1_Letter_es.pdf — the Letter (9 May 2026). The work itself, and the document that makes the claim now withdrawn. Unmodified.
  2. 2_Erratum_v2_en.pdf — the v2.0 erratum (11 May 2026) on scheme dependence. Superseded by the corrigendum below, and English only.
  3. 3_Corrigendum_v3_en.pdf / 3_Corrigendum_v3_es.pdf — the corrigendum (12 July 2026). This is the current status of the work.

What is withdrawn. The closed form reported in v1 and v2 is a corollary of Eq. (4.2) of A. Helset, A. Martin and M. Trott, The Geometric Standard Model Effective Field Theory, JHEP 03 (2020) 163 (arXiv:2001.01453). Their §4.1 already gives the three- and four-point functions of the scalar potential to all orders in v/Λ. At dimension six both are linear in a single Wilson coefficient, and eliminating it between them yields the trilinear–quartic relation in one line of algebra. Once the field-space metric is divided out, that relation is affine, with integer coefficients, and contains no α whatsoever: the rational function of α reported in v1/v2 was never in the physics, it came entirely from metric prefactors that had not been divided out. The basis dependence that the v2.0 erratum reported as a discovery is likewise stated in advance, in Appendix B of that same 2020 paper.

What stands. The algebra of the Letter and its Lean verification remain correct — they verify a corollary, not a discovery. Separately, the audit that produced the Letter also found a typesetting error in the paper it was auditing, and that finding is unaffected: line 310 of arXiv:2504.05974 prints δκ₄ = δκ₃/6 where the paper's own equations give δκ₄ = 6 δκ₃, a factor of 36. It was reported to the authors and is still in the published version.

Why the retraction is in the record rather than a deletion. The Letter was circulated, cited to its authors by name, and read. Removing it would leave the claim in circulation with nothing attached to it. It stays, with the withdrawal in front of it.
