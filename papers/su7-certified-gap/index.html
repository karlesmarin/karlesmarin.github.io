Abstract

A ceiling answers “how high can it go”. Running the same map backwards answers “give me a content at this scale, or show me there is none” — and the second question has an answer the first cannot see.

Part VII converts the one-loop Wilson potential of the SU(7) model of Komori and Maru (arXiv:2503.04090) into an integer programme over the bulk-content lattice and certifies 1/R₅ ≤ 10.03 TeV with an exact rational dual. That is an extremal statement, and it has no use for a lower end. This paper runs the map the other way. Given a target box in the two observables the closed form predicts, 1/R₅ and mh, it either exhibits a bulk content or returns a certificate with a name that none exists — cone, congruence, an exact rational Farkas pair, a finite exhaustion, or a floor: the inverse map can ask for an A₄ below that of the empty content, which non-negative multiplicities forbid.

What comes out is not an interval. A content fixes its two observables outright, with no continuous parameter left, so the image of any finite part of the lattice is a finite set of points. On the published gauge seed they fall into one cluster per rung of the quantised curvature — 35 points spanning [8897.5, 9967] GeV at 8D = 1, 65 spanning [5258, 6215] at 8D = 3 — and between the top of one and the bottom of the other there is nothing at all over 2682 GeV, forty-five times the widest gap inside either cluster. That stretch is closed above by rational dual certificates and below by an enumeration, not observed by a search. At the measured Higgs mass what is left is a single point, 1/R₅ = 8967.5 GeV, on which 81 distinct contents coincide — a single one-loop potential built 81 ways, the smallest of 19 multiplets and the largest of 198.

And the map does not determine everything. Two bulk contents share both closed-form observables exactly and disagree about the vacuum, so W — the invariant whose sign can rule the electroweak point out — is not a function of the two numbers the model is fitted to. On the untruncated one-loop potential that pair parts by 3.8 MeV in mh, a factor of 29 under the quoted 1σ experimental uncertainty.

Scope, stated rather than implied. Every emptiness statement here, the hole included, is a statement about the closed form the two identities define, not yet about the untruncated model: turning it into one needs a uniform remainder bound carried through the certificate, which is open and is said to be open. Unless a number is labelled exact, every 1/R₅ and mh displayed is a closed-form value; the content at every cluster end and every delivered design is re-minimised on the exact potential, which throughout means the untruncated one-loop Li₅ potential and not an all-orders effective one. Where the enumerator runs out of its declared budget the verdict printed is undecided, not a no-go that has not been earned.

Both editions, English and Spanish, carry the same equations, numbers and labels. Every number regenerates from the ancillary scripts, whose stdout is archived beside them, and the stdout of each of the eight gates that were green is archived with them.
